Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data DOLMI Slovakia, s.r.o. collects, how it is used, and what rights you have under applicable data protection laws.

Effective date: 1 January 2026 Last updated: 1 January 2026

01. Who We Are

This website is operated by DOLMI Slovakia, s.r.o., a company registered in the Slovak Republic, with registered office at:

Karpatské námestie 10, 831 06 Bratislava, Slovakia

We provide full-cycle digital studio services, including brand systems, website design and development, landing pages, performance marketing, SEO systems, analytics support and website care to companies across Europe and internationally.

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and applicable Slovak data protection legislation, DOLMI Slovakia, s.r.o. acts as the data controller in respect of personal data collected through this website.

We are committed to protecting the personal data of website visitors, prospective clients and business partners. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and what rights you have.

02. Data We Collect

We may collect personal data when you interact with our website, submit a form, communicate with us, or engage our services.

The types of data we may collect include:

  • Identity data — name, surname, company name and job title.
  • Contact data — email address and business address.
  • Financial data — invoicing details, VAT numbers and billing-related information where applicable.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information, time zone and system data.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, navigation paths and interactions.
  • Marketing data — preferences for receiving communications and campaign-related interactions.
  • Business data — information shared in relation to services, such as project goals, budgets, timeline, website links, campaign needs or project scope.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data. If such data is provided, it will be deleted where appropriate unless retention is legally required.

03. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to inquiries and contact requests submitted through the website.
  • To provide, manage and improve our services.
  • To manage client relationships and business communications.
  • To prepare proposals, scopes of work, invoices and business records.
  • To analyse website performance and user behaviour.
  • To support marketing, advertising and remarketing activities where permitted.
  • To ensure website security and prevent misuse.
  • To comply with legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

We do not use your personal data for unrelated purposes without informing you where required.

04. Legal Basis for Processing

We rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — when processing is necessary to provide requested services or take steps before entering into a contract.
  • Legitimate interests — to improve services, respond to business enquiries, ensure website security and operate our business.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable laws, accounting rules and regulatory requirements.
  • Consent — where required, including for certain marketing communications or non-essential cookies.

You may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

05. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

  • Client and financial data — up to 10 years where required for accounting, tax or legal purposes.
  • Inquiry data — up to 2 years after the last relevant communication.
  • Analytics data — up to 26 months where analytics tools are enabled.
  • Marketing data — until consent is withdrawn or the data is no longer needed.

We may anonymise data for analytical or service improvement purposes.

06. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share data with:

  • Service providers, such as hosting providers, email tools, CRM systems, form processing tools and analytics services.
  • Advertising platforms, such as Google and Meta, where advertising or tracking tools are enabled and permitted.
  • Professional advisers, including legal, accounting or tax advisers.
  • Authorities where disclosure is required by law.

All third parties are expected to handle personal data securely and lawfully.

07. International Transfers

Some providers, including Google or Meta, may process data outside the European Economic Area.

Where this occurs, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), approved data transfer mechanisms or other measures required under applicable data protection law.

08. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Restrict processing of your data.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky).

09. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, analyse usage and support marketing efforts where enabled. For full details, please refer to our Cookie Policy.
