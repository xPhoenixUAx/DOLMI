Service logic

Launch is not the finish line. It is where useful data starts.

After a website goes live, the real operational needs appear: content changes, campaign pages, form improvements, technical checks, speed adjustments and new business priorities. We organize that work into a manageable support flow.

Core focus areas

These are the practical parts of the service that usually shape the scope. We adapt the depth of each item to the project stage, budget and business priority.

Questions

Useful details before starting

Is this only for websites Dolmi built?

No. We can support existing websites too, although the first step is usually a technical and content review.

Can support be monthly?

Yes. Support can be monthly or sprint-based depending on how often the site and campaigns need changes.

Can you add new landing pages after launch?

Yes. This is one of the most common reasons to keep support active after the initial website release.
