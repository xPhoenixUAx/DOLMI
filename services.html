FAQ

Before we start, these are the questions clients usually ask.

Short answers for planning scope, timing and communication before a detailed proposal is prepared.

Can we order only one service?

Yes. You can start with a landing page, website redesign, SEO structure or campaign support, then extend the scope later if needed.

How do you estimate the project price?

We look at page count, design depth, development logic, integrations, content readiness and launch support. The final quote is confirmed by a manager after the brief.

Do you handle design and development together?

Yes. Dolmi can cover the full chain from structure and visual design to frontend, backend, forms, deployment and post-launch updates.

Can you support paid campaigns after launch?

Yes. We can prepare landing pages, tracking, analytics events and optimization cycles for Google, Meta and other advertising channels.
