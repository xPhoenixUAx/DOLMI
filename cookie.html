Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how DOLMI Slovakia, s.r.o. uses cookies and similar technologies across the website available at dolmidigital.com.

Effective date: 1 January 2026 Last updated: 1 January 2026

01. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites work more efficiently, improve user experience, and provide website operators with information about how the site is used.

Cookies can be session cookies, which are deleted when you close your browser, or persistent cookies, which remain on your device for a set period of time or until you delete them manually. Cookies may be set by the website you are visiting or by third-party services integrated into that website.

In addition to cookies, we may use similar technologies such as pixels, local storage objects and scripts for similar purposes. References to cookies in this policy include these similar tracking technologies.

This Cookie Policy forms part of our Privacy Policy and should be read together with it. Where required under the EU ePrivacy Directive and the GDPR, non-essential cookies are used only with appropriate consent.

02. Why We Use Cookies

  • To support basic website functionality, including navigation, form submission and security measures.
  • To remember preferences where preference tools are available.
  • To analyse how visitors use the website so we can improve its structure, content and performance.
  • To measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and understand which channels drive traffic to the site.
  • To support remarketing or retargeting activities where such tools are enabled and consent has been provided.
  • To comply with applicable advertising platform policies, including conversion tracking requirements.

03. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot usually be switched off. They are set in response to actions such as submitting forms or setting privacy preferences.

Analytics Cookies help us understand visits and website interaction. Where enabled, analytics data is used to improve the website experience. You may opt out of analytics cookies without affecting basic site access.

Marketing Cookies may be set by advertising partners to measure campaigns and show relevant advertisements on other platforms. You may opt out of marketing cookies at any time where such cookies are used.

05. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services. We do not control these cookies directly, and they are governed by the privacy policies of the respective third parties.

The key third-party providers whose cookies may be present on our website include Google LLC for Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion tracking and Google Tag Manager, and Meta Platforms Ireland Limited for Meta Pixel advertising and audience creation.

These third parties may use data collected through their cookies for their own purposes, including personalised advertising, subject to their policies and your consent choices.

06. Managing Your Preferences

  • Browser settings: you can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Google Ads settings: adssettings.google.com.
  • Meta ad preferences: facebook.com/adpreferences.

Withdrawing consent for non-essential cookies does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing. Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled if they are required for the website to function.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation or our services. Any material updates will be communicated on our website where required.
